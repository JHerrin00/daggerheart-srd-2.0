# Infinite Staff

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Physical_ _Weapon_

*Presence* · *Melee* · *d10+9 phy* · *Two-Handed*

### FEATURE

***Extending:*** You can increase the range of this weapon up to Very Far. You gain a −1 penalty to attack rolls for each step you increase the range by (such as Melee to Very Close, Very Close to Close, or Close to Far).
