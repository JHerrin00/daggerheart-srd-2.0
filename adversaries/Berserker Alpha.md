# Berserker Alpha

***Tier 2 Leader***
*A veteran commander who quaffs magic elixirs to change into a beast and lead their packmates on a hunt.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Embrace the change, howl at the moon, lead the pack, run down enemies

> **Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 13/26 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 4  
> **ATK:** +3 | **Tooth & Claw:** Melee | 2d10+2 phy

**Experience:** Hunt +3, Keen Senses +2

## FEATURES

***Pack Fury - Passive:*** The Alpha gains a +1 bonus to their attack rolls for each ally within Very Close range of their attack’s target.

***Bark at the Moon - Action:*** Once per scene, **spend a Fear** to have each ally within Close range of the Alpha clear a HP and a Stress.

***Berserker Rage - Reaction:*** The first time in a scene the Alpha marks their last Stress, they gain a +6 bonus to damage rolls.
