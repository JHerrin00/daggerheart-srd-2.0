# Will-O’-The-Wisps

***Tier 1 Horde (8/HP)***
*Lazily floating orbs of color-shifting werelight.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Dazzle and distract, disorient, lead astray

> **Difficulty:** 9 | **Thresholds:** 5/9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2  
> **ATK:** −3 | **Flash:** Close | 1d4+2 mag

**Experience:** Darkness +3

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d4−1) - Passive:*** When the Will-o’-the-Wisps have marked half or more of their HP, their standard attack deals **1d4−1** magic damage instead.

***Kaleidoscopic - Passive:*** The Will-o’-the-Wisps pulse and dance in hypnotic, color-changing patterns that entrance any creature who looks upon them. All targets within Very Close range of the Will-o’-the-Wisps are *Vulnerable.*

***Fascinating - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Will-o’-the-Wisps trace looping light trails through the air, leaving afterimages that beg to be deciphered. Each PC within Far range must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll (13) or mark a Stress and move up to Close range toward the Will-o’-the-Wisps.
