# Gargoyle

***Tier 2 Bruiser***
*A magically animated statue resembling a terrifying beast or demon.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Hide in plain sight, overwatch, swoop down

> **Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 14/28 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 2  
> **ATK:** +2 | **Stone Claws:** Melee | 2d10+3 phy

**Experience:** Camouflage +4, Ruins +3

## FEATURES

***Unliving - Passive:*** The Gargoyle has resistance to magic damage.

***Swooping Strike - Action:*** Choose a point within Far range. The Gargoyle moves to that point and makes a standard attack against one target along its path.

***Petrifying Slash - Reaction:*** When the Gargoyle deals damage, the target must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or become *Vulnerable*. If the target is already *Vulnerable,* they become *Restrained*. If the target is already *Vulnerable* and *Restrained,* they must make a death move. The PC can spend a Hope per condition to clear it.
