# Whisper Wraith

***Tier 3 Skulk***
*The echoes of broken promises and dreams unfulfilled, manifesting as a floating, translucent shroud of tattered black velvet in a vaguely humanoid shape.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bind limbs, drop from a great height, lift off the ground, swirl through the air, wrap around heads

> **Difficulty:** 16 | **Thresholds:** 20/32 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 6  
> **ATK:** +4 | **Shadow Touch:** Melee | 3d8+4 mag

**Experience:** Dreams +4

## FEATURES

***Greater Specter - Passive:*** The Wraith has resistance to physical damage and can move through solid objects.

***Spooky - Passive:*** When the Wraith makes a successful attack, all PCs within Close range must mark a Stress, and you gain a Fear.

***Nightmare Shroud - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Wraith wrap itself around a PC within Melee range, *Shrouding* the target until they succeed on a Strength Roll to dislodge the Wraith or the Wraith takes Major or greater damage. While *Shrouded,* the target has disadvantage on attack rolls and marks a Stress when they roll with Fear. Additionally, damage that would be dealt to the Wraith is split evenly between the Wraith and the *Shrouded* target.
