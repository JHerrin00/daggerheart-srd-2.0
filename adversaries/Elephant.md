# Elephant

***Tier 2 Bruiser***
*A huge pachyderm that has large ears and a prehensile trunk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Eat vegetation, protect the herd, socialize

> **Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 14/28 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3  
> **ATK:** +3 | **Feet & Tusks:** Very Close | 2d12 phy

**Experience:** Huge +2, Never Forgets +2

## FEATURES

***Trample - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to have the Elephant charge up to Far range in a straight line. Each target in its path must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll or take **2d10+3** physical damage.

***Toss - Reaction:*** When the Elephant makes a successful standard attack, you can **spend a Fear** to have it toss the target to another location within Close range and deal an extra **1d12** damage.
