# Urco

***Tier 2 Standard***
*A black-furred hound from beyond the veil of death that portends death and feeds on fear.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Feed, frighten, hunt

> **Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3  
> **ATK:** +1 | **Black Fang:** Melee | 2d8+4 mag

**Experience:** Omens +3, Stealth +3

## FEATURES

***Fearmonger - Passive:*** When a PC within Very Close range fails with Hope, you gain a Fear.

***Terror-Fueled - Passive:*** The Urco gains a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to the number of Fear you have.

***Fear Eater - Action:*** **Spend any number of Fear** to clear the same number of Stress. If the Urco has no marked Stress, you can **spend any number of Fear** to clear the same number of HP.

***Baleful Gaze - Reaction:*** When the Urco takes damage from an attack, you can **spend a Fear** to cause the attacker’s damage thresholds to gain a −1 penalty until the end of the scene. This effect can stack.
