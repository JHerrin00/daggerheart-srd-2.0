# Darkweave Spinner

***Tier 1 Standard***
*A boar-sized arachnid woven from primordial shadow-silk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bite, stick to the darkness

> **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 6/9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3  
> **ATK:** +1 | **Fangs:** Melee | 1d8+1 phy

**Experience:** Holes +2, Webs +2

## FEATURES

***Wrap in Shadow-Silk - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the target is *Vulnerable* and *Restrained* until they succeed on a Strength Roll (10).

***Shadow Fang - Reaction:*** When the Spinner makes a successful attack against a target within Melee range, you can **spend a Fear** to make the target *Shaky* until they succeed on an Instinct Roll (10). While *Shaky*, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls.
