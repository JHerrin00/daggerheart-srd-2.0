# Rust Eater

***Tier 2 Standard***
*A pony-sized cockroach coated in a corrosive chitin that turns metal into rust.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid detection, eat rust, sniff out metal

> **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 10/18 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 2  
> **ATK:** +1 | **Bite:** Melee | 2d6+4 phy

**Experience:** Snuffle +3

## FEATURES

***Stifling Pheromones - Passive:*** A PC who makes an action roll within Very Close range of the Rust Eater must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll or mark a Stress.

***Rust Touch - Passive:*** The Rust Eater’s carapace is coated in a corrosive acid that pits and rusts metal. When a PC within Melee range of the Rust Easter succeeds on an attack against it with a metal weapon, reduce the weapon’s damage die by one step (for example, d8 to d6 or d6 to d4). If a weapon’s damage die is reduced below a d4, the weapon crumbles into rust. The Rust Eater then devours the rust pile and clears a HP.
