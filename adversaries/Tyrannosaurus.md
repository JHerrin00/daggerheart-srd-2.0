# Tyrannosaurus

***Tier 2 Bruiser***
*A huge carnivorous dinosaur that walks on two legs.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Break through the treeline, eat, stomp around

> **Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **Stress:** 5  
> **ATK:** +2 | **Mighty Jaws:** Very Close | 2d10+4 phy

**Experience:** Predator +3

## FEATURES

***Earth-Shaking Roar - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to force each PC within Close range to make a Presence Reaction Roll. On a failure, they must choose to lose a Hope or mark **1d4** Stress.

***Stomp - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes **3d12** direct physical damage.

***Crushing Bite - Reaction:*** When the Tyrannosaurus makes a successful standard attack, you can **spend a Fear** to force the target to mark an additional HP.
