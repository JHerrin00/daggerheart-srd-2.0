# Redcap Skinner

***Tier 1 Minion***
*A cat-sized fey creature wielding a straight razor and wearing a conical red cap sealed on their head with candle wax.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Flense, giggle, scrape, slice

> **Difficulty:** 9 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 1  
> **ATK:** −1 | **Razor:** Melee | 2 phy

**Experience:** Leatherwork +3

## FEATURES

***Minion (4) - Passive:*** The Skinner is defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Skinner, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Shallow Cuts - Passive:*** When a PC fails a roll, they take 1 physical damage for each Skinner within Melee range of them. Combine this damage.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to choose a target and spotlight all Redcap Skinners within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
