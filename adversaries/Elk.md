# Elk

***Tier 1 Skulk***
*A large deer.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bound away, graze, rut, sense danger

> **Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **Stress:** 2  
> **ATK:** +0 | **Antlers:** Melee | 1d8+1 phy

**Experience:** Keen Senses +3, Run +3

## FEATURES

***Headbutt - Passive:*** When the Elk moves from Close range or farther before making a standard attack, it deals **1d12+2** physical damage instead of its standard damage.

***Bolt - Reaction:*** When the Elk marks a HP or Stress, it must succeed on a Reaction Roll (10) or flee the scene.
