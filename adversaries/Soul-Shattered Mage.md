# Soul-Shattered Mage

***Tier 1 Solo***
*A spellcaster with a psyche broken by knowledge best left hidden.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Babble in an unknown language, laugh unsettlingly, wield power beyond control

> **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 8/15 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 5  
> **ATK:** +2 | **Wormwood Staff :** Far | 1d12 mag

**Experience:** Forbidden Knowledge +2

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Mage can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

***Broken Magic - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to roll a **d6**. The Mage uses the corresponding move: 1–2: ***Dimension Rift.*** Choose a target within Far range. The target teleports to a point you choose within Close range of their original position and takes **2d6** direct magic damage as their body and mind reassemble. 3–4: ***Time Dilation.*** Choose a group of PCs within Far range. Each target is *Slowed* until they roll with Hope. While *Slowed*, a target is *Vulnerable* and must mark a Stress when they move. 5–6: ***Discordant Visions.*** Make an attack against up to three targets within Close range. Targets the Mage succeeds against take **2d8** direct magic damage.

***Feel My Pain - Reaction:*** When the Mage marks HP, roll a number of **d6s** equal to the number of Stress the Mage has marked. The Mage deals direct magic damage equal to the total result to all PCs within Very Close range.
