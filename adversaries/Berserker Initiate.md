# Berserker Initiate

***Tier 2 Standard***
*A raging warrior who imbibes ritualistic concoctions to enhance their form with aspects of a predatory beast.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Coordinate with packmates, dance around the fire, howl at the moon

> **Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3  
> **ATK:** +1 | **Tooth & Claw:** Melee | 2d10 phy

**Experience:** Hunt +3

## FEATURES

***Pack Fury - Passive:*** The Initiate gains a +1 bonus to their attack rolls for each ally within Very Close range of their attack’s target.

***Berserker Rage - Reaction:*** The first time in a scene the Initiate marks their last Stress, they gain a +4 bonus to damage rolls.
