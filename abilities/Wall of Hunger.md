# Wall of Hunger

***Level 7*** *Dread Spell.* ***Recall Cost*** *2.*

Make a **Spellcast Roll (10)**. On a success, you can **spend a Hope** to create a visible wall of writhing necrotic energy between two points within Far range. The wall lasts until you mark a Hit Point or cast this spell again. A creature inside the wall when it appears or that passes through it must mark 2 Stress.
