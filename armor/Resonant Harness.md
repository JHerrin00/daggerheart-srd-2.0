# Resonant Harness
**_Tier 4_** _Armor_

* *Base Thresholds:* 15 / 40  
* *Base Score:* 7

### FEATURE

***Vitreous:*** When you would take Severe or greater damage, you can mark 2 Armor Slots to negate that damage. If you do, you gain a −5 penalty to your damage thresholds until you choose to repair your armor as a downtime move.
