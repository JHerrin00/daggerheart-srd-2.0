difficulty: 16
impulses: "Draw them deep, drown them, reveal ancient wonders"
name: "Sunken Citadel"
potential_adversaries: "Cursed Merfolk, Deep Dweller, *Plesiosaurus*"
tier: 3
type: "Exploration"
feats:
  - name: "Underwater Environment - Passive"
    text: "This environment is underwater (see the \"Moving and Fighting Underwater\" rules on page 168 of the core rulebook). In addition to those rules, a creature who breathes air tracks their supply with an Air Supply Countdown. Its starting value is equal to 3 + their tier, and it ticks down when they take an action. If a PC fails an action roll or rolls with Fear, you can use a GM move to tick down their countdown an additional time. If the roll is a failure with Fear, you can tick it down twice instead. Once their countdown triggers, a PC must mark a Stress whenever they take an action underwater until they resurface.\n\n_Have you ever known anyone who drowned? What do you think about to keep calm and stretch your air supply?_"
  - name: "Air Pockets - Passive"
    text: "A PC can reset their their Air Supply Countdown to its starting value by locating a pocket of air with a successful Knowledge or Instinct Roll.\n\n_What signs or structures do you look for? What does it feel like to know this might be the last breath you take?_"
  - name: "Lost Technology - Passive"
    text: "The ruins are carved with strange magical runes and littered with lost technology. A PC can make a Knowledge Roll to learn useful information about this lost civilization from any runes or devices they come across.\n\n_Who once called this place home? How did their technology work? Why were they ultimately destroyed and cast down into the depths?_"
  - name: "Deepwater Denizens - Action"
    text: "**Spend a Fear** to summon up to two Deep Dwellers and **1d6** Cursed Merfolk who try to bind the PCs to their will. These adversaries appear at Close range of a PC, then one immediately takes the spotlight.\n\n_Why do these creatures stay here among the ruins? Are they unwilling or unable to resurrect this once-prosperous civilization?_"
