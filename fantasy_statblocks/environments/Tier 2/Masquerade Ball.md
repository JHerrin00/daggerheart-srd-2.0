    text: "A very important person confuses a PC with an even more important person. The target can play into the misunderstanding with a Presence Reaction Roll. On a failure, the PC marks a Stress as their true identity is discovered, and they are threatened with ejection from the party.\n\n_Who does the VIP mistake them for? What doors does their mistake open? What dangers does it bring?_"
  - name: "Cloak and Dagger - Action"
    text: "**Spend a Fear** to have an operative try to steal something valuable or assassinate someone important. On a failure with Fear, the PC is erroneously implicated in the crime.\n\n_Who does the operative work for? Why did they send them to the masquerade?_"
