  - name: "R&R - Passive"

When the PCs rest here, they gain an additional downtime move, the GM doesn't gain Fear, and no long-term countdowns advance.\n\n_What's so relaxing about this place? Does it remind you of somewhere you've been before?_"
  - name: "\"Our Story So Far…\" - Passive"
    text: "Once per scene when a PC recounts a memorable moment from the campaign in conversation with another PC, they both gain a Hope.\n\n_What sounds, smells, or sensations does the memory conjure in your mind? How did that moment permanently change you—or your opinion of your companion?_"
  - name: "Local Trouble - Action"
    text: "**Spend a Fear** to interrupt a PC's respite with a minor complication, such as a gang of surly beach bums, an overly exuberant fan, or a really bad sunburn. The PC must gracefully resolve the situation or mark a Stress from the resulting embarrassment or frustration.\n\n_Why does this PC draw their attention? What do the locals care about more than these newly arrived adventurers?_"
  - name: "Fashion Show - Reaction"
    text: "When the party prepares for their first day at the beach, have each player describe how their PC changes their attire to be more appropriate for the environment. The GM gives the PC with the freshest look a **d6** Fresh Die that can be spent to add to any action or reaction roll.\n\n_What's the fashion in this area? Where do the PCs source their new beach-themed articles and accessories?_"
